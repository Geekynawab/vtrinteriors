Our Story

Crafting spaces since 2021

Founder Photo — Coming Soon

VTR structured itself as a company after completion of 100+ projects by designer Supriyaa Sista alias Sunitha Priya with her experience in the field of design. A 1996 batch designer, during her journey with Architects and companies she designed offices, banks, showrooms and residences. Her collaboration with companies gave her insightful knowledge of market and sales.

The logo VTR was developed with the intention of providing qualitative work, from paper to reality — Visual To Reality. Bringing images to realistic forms, providing customers with visual contentment and bliss.

VTR has a team of designers, Engineers and Architects who, by sketching the dream of the customer, craft spaces that are more functional and have an aesthetic appeal and ambience.

Design Philosophy

Our design philosophy works on the principle of form following function. Design is not about making beautiful spaces — it is about manipulating the environment to improve human experience and behaviour. It bridges the gap between art and architecture; the space we inhabit deeply shapes how we think, feel and function.
