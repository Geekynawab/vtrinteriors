Our Story

Crafting spaces since 2021

Founder Photo Coming Soon

VTR Interiors was established on the foundation of 100+ completed projects, bringing together a team of designers, engineers, and architects united by a single vision: transforming spaces from concept to reality.

Founded by Supriyaa Sista, a designer since 1996 with experience across offices, banks, showrooms, and residences, VTR was built on deep industry knowledge gained through collaborations with leading architects and companies. That experience shaped our understanding of both design and the market.

The name VTR, Visual To Reality, defines our purpose: turning ideas on paper into spaces that are functional, aesthetic, and alive. We work with clients to sketch their dream and craft environments that bring visual contentment and a lasting sense of place.

Design Philosophy

Our design philosophy works on the principle of form following function. Design is not about making beautiful spaces. It is about manipulating the environment to improve human experience and behaviour. It bridges the gap between art and architecture; the space we inhabit deeply shapes how we think, feel and function.
